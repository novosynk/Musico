# MUSICO

### Listen Ad-Free. Enjoy High-Quality Music.

Musico is a modern music listening experience designed around one simple idea: music should feel effortless.

Search for the music you love, discover something new, press play and enjoy a clean, uninterrupted listening experience with high-quality audio, powerful discovery, beautiful playback controls and offline listening.

Musico is built for people who want their music experience to feel simple, fast, personal and premium.

---

## The Musico Experience

Musico brings music discovery and listening together in one focused experience.

From the moment the application opens, everything is designed around listening.

No unnecessary complexity.

No distracting interface.

Just your music, your library and your listening experience.

Search for a song.

Press play.

Enjoy.

---

## Ad-Free Listening

Musico is designed around an uninterrupted listening experience.

The interface should remain clean and focused on music rather than advertisements, promotional interruptions or unnecessary distractions.

The goal is simple:

**Open Musico. Choose your music. Listen.**

---

## High-Quality Music

Music should sound as good as the available source allows.

Musico prioritizes high-quality playback, with **320 kbps as the default preferred quality whenever it is genuinely available**.

If a song does not provide 320 kbps, Musico automatically uses the best available quality rather than preventing playback.

Users can choose their preferred playback quality from Settings.

The experience should always prioritize:

**Best available quality. Reliable playback.**

## Multiple Music Sources

Musico is designed to bring music discovery from multiple supported music services into one experience.

Supported and planned music sources include:

**YouTube Music**

Search and discover music from YouTube Music and play the exact selected track whenever a playable stream is available.

**Spotify**

Search Spotify music and discover tracks, artists, albums and playlists through Spotify's available services.

Spotify discovery and playback are treated as separate capabilities, allowing Musico to provide the best possible listening experience without pretending that unavailable Spotify audio streams exist.

**JioSaavn**

Discover and listen to music available through JioSaavn, including high-quality streams where available.

**Audius**

Discover music and listen to tracks available through the Audius platform.

**Jamendo**

Discover and listen to music available through the Jamendo catalog.

**Local Music**

Listen to music already stored on the device.

Musico should treat every source as part of one unified listening experience rather than making users switch between different applications.

---

## Exact Music Playback

When a user searches for a specific song and selects a specific result, Musico should play that exact track.

The application should preserve the selected song's identity and avoid silently replacing it with:

- A different recording
- A cover
- A remix
- A live version
- Another artist
- An unrelated search result

If a playback source cannot provide the selected track, Musico should handle the situation transparently rather than quietly playing something else.

The principle is simple:

**You choose the song. Musico plays that song.**

---

## Intelligent Playback

Music sources can behave differently.

Some provide discovery.

Some provide metadata.

Some provide direct audio streaming.

Some provide different audio qualities.

Musico is designed to handle these differences intelligently while keeping the listening experience simple.

If the preferred playback method is temporarily unavailable, Musico can use another supported method when the exact track can be reliably matched.

The user should not have to understand how the music source works.

They should simply press play and listen.

## Offline Listening

Music should not disappear when the internet does.

The experience should be simple:

**Download once. Listen anywhere.**

When a downloaded version of a track exists, Musico should prefer the local copy so the user can continue listening without relying on an internet connection.
