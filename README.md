## Overview

**Musico** is built around one clear philosophy: **music should feel effortless.**

Traditional streaming apps are often cluttered with disruptive audio ads, paid restrictions on background playback, and intrusive user tracking. Musico removes the distractions and provides a clean, fast, and high-quality listening experience directly on your Android device.

> **Clean. Fast. Ad-Free. Pure Music Experience.**

---

## Key Features

<div align="center">

| Feature | Specification | Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ad-Free Playback** | `Zero Ads` | Completely uninterrupted playback without audio sponsors or banner interruptions. |
| **High-Fidelity Audio** | `320 kbps` | Prioritized high-definition audio streaming for crystal-clear acoustics. |
| **Offline Downloads** | `Local Storage` | Download songs and playlists locally to enjoy playback without an internet connection. |
| **Synchronized Lyrics** | `Live Sync` | Real-time synchronized lyrics viewer that highlights lines as the track plays. |
| **Lightning Search** | `Instant Search` | Rapid search across tracks, artists, albums, and curated playlists. |
| **Exact Track Playback** | `Direct Match` | Plays the exact selected song without unwanted remixes or low-quality cover substitutions. |
| **Library & Playlists** | `Custom Library` | Organize favorites, create custom playlists, and access your recent listening history. |
| **Adaptive Modern UI** | `Dark Theme` | Minimal dark surface with fluid transitions and responsive layouts. |
| **Sleep Timer & Queue** | `Smart Controls` | Built-in sleep timer for nighttime listening and full playback queue management. |
| **Privacy & FOSS** | `MIT License` | Open-source software with zero telemetry, data collection, or mandatory accounts. |

</div>

### Installation Steps
1. Open the [Latest Releases](https://github.com/novosynk/Musico/releases/latest) page.
2. Download the latest `.apk` package (e.g., `Musico-vX.X.X.apk`).
3. Open the downloaded file from your device notifications or file manager.
4. If prompted, grant permission to install applications from your browser or file manager.
5. Tap **Install** and launch **Musico**.

## Interface & Experience

- **Mini Player** `Persistent Bar`: Convenient playback bar for continuous music control while browsing libraries and charts.
- **Immersive Full Player** `Full Screen`: Large cover art display, precision timeline scrubber, live lyrics viewer, and audio controls.
- **Moods & Discovery** `Dynamic Channels`: Curated stations tailored to your current vibe—Deep Focus, Chill, Night Drive, Workout, and more.
- **Distraction-Free Audio** `Pure Sound`: Designed strictly around music without clutter or unwanted promotional feeds.
